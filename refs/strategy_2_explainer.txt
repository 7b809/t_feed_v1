================================================================================
STRATEGY 2 — NORMAL DAY / FALLBACK STRATEGY
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Trade normal / range-bound days when Strategy 1's conditions are NOT met:
no clear R2 breakout in the first 5 minutes, low opening momentum, range
behaviour. Instead of forcing a trade off an R2 break that never happens,
Strategy 2 looks at a deeper pullback on the option premium (isolation level
touch) and waits for a fresh EMA cross on that same contract to confirm
direction.

KEY DESIGN: STRATEGY 2 REUSES STAGE 1 AND STAGE 2 OF STRATEGY 1
----------------------------------------------------------------
  * Stage 1 (buffer filter + history fetch + last-day cross) -> unchanged.
  * Stage 2 (EMA isolation + CE/PE pairing)                   -> unchanged.
  * Only Stage 3 and Stage 4 are replaced.

PIPELINE (new Stage 3 and Stage 4)
-----------------------------------
Stage 3 — Isolation Level Touch Filter (S3 by default)
  * Take all Stage 2 isolated contracts.
  * For each contract, read its OWN opening-range S3 level
    (computed from its own 09:15 1-min candle).
  * Watch for any contract whose price touches/crosses its own S3
    at any time during the day (09:15 - 15:30).
  * Any contract that touches its own S3 becomes a Stage 3 candidate.
  * Reduce candidates to ONE final isolated instrument:
        if exactly one candidate -> that is the pick
        if multiple candidates:
            1) filter by ticker priority
               (default: NIFTY preferred over SENSEX; configurable)
            2) within chosen ticker: pick the strike nearest to
               that index's ATM (|strike - index LTP| minimum)
  * Isolation level is configurable (default = S3; can be S2/R2/R3).
  * Output:
      data/strategy2/stage_3_isolation_level/
          stage_3_summary.json
          <TICKER>/<INDEX>/<strike>_<TYPE>/s3_touch.json

Stage 4 — Next-Cross Order Placement
  * Wait for the NEXT EMA cross on the final isolated instrument
    that occurs AFTER its S3 touch.
  * Ignore earlier crosses.
  * Trigger on that cross:
        bullish -> BUY the same CE at that cross price
        bearish -> BUY the opposite PE (same strike, opposite type)
                   at the same timestamp
  * Price for opposite-type order: sibling PE's own LTP at that same
    timestamp (to be confirmed).
  * Paper order only.
  * Output:
      data/strategy2/stage_4_order/
          stage_4_summary.json
          <TICKER>/<INDEX>/orders.json
          order_meta_test.json

WORKED EXAMPLE (from the 16-10-2026 data)
------------------------------------------
Instrument: NIFTY 22850 CE
First 09:15 candle: H=253.9, L=205.05
  A      = (H+L)/2      = 229.475
  D_H    = |H-A|        = 24.425
  D_L    = |A-L|        = 24.425
  S3     = L - 2*D_L    = 156.20

Event timeline:
  09:15  first candle
  09:27  low = 155.65 <= 156.20   -> S3 TOUCHED (candidate)
  09:53  bullish cross (valid)    -> NEXT cross after S3 touch
                                      -> BUY 22850 CE at 09:53 price

Alternate scenario:
  If the 09:53 cross were BEARISH
    -> BUY 22850 PE at the 09:53 timestamp
       (bearish on CE = underlying weakening = PE gains)

WHY THIS WORKS ON NORMAL DAYS
-----------------------------
  * S3 sits well below the opening range — only tested on a real
    intraday pullback, not on the standard R2 breakout.
  * Waiting for the FIRST cross after S3 touch means the trade is
    triggered by a fresh momentum shift, not by the touch itself.
  * If momentum resumes up    -> buy the CE that got isolated.
  * If momentum reverses down -> buy the opposite PE of the same strike,
    i.e. express the bearish read on the same underlying level.
  * Picking the ATM-nearest strike keeps the entry liquid and the
    paper-fill realistic.

KEY DIFFERENCES vs STRATEGY 1
------------------------------
  Aspect              | Strategy 1           | Strategy 2
  --------------------|----------------------|----------------------
  Trigger level       | own R2               | own S3 (default)
  Time window         | 09:15 - 09:20        | 09:15 - 15:30
  Order rule          | nearest ATM + FIFO   | next cross after S3
  Direction rule      | BUY isolated type    | bullish -> same,
                      |                      | bearish -> opposite
  Best on             | high-momentum days   | normal/range days
  Selection priority  | per-index N picks    | 1 ticker, 1 strike
                      | then FIFO            | nearest to ATM

CONFIG FLAGS (proposed)
-----------------------
  STRATEGY_2_ENABLED                 true
  STRATEGY_2_ISOLATION_LEVEL         S3   (can be S2/R2/R3)
  STRATEGY_2_TICKER_PRIORITY         NIFTY,SENSEX
  STRATEGY_2_TRACKER_POLL_SECONDS    1
  STRATEGY_2_ORDER_QTY               1
