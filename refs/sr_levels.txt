# Support / Resistance Levels — Calculation & File Format Spec

This document defines **how support and resistance levels are computed**
and **exactly how they are persisted** in `sr_levels.json`. It is the
single source of truth for the algorithm, the record shape, the
directory layout, and the invariants downstream consumers can rely on.

Anything that writes `sr_levels.json` — the Python pipeline, a manual
fix, a future Rust/Go port — must produce a file that satisfies every
rule below.

---

## 1. What a support / resistance level is

A **resistance** is a swing high. A **support** is a swing low. Both
are detected with the classic *pivot* rule used by TradingView's
`ta.pivothigh` / `ta.pivotlow` and by the Pine Script indicator
*Support and Resistance Levels with Breaks* (LuxAlgo, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

Let `left_bars` and `right_bars` be two positive integers (defaults:
`15` and `15`).

Given a 1-minute candle series `C[0..n-1]` where each candle has
`high`, `low`, `open`, `close`, `volume`, `timestamp`:

- Candle `i` is a **resistance pivot** iff:
  - `C[i].high > C[j].high` for every `j` in `[i - left_bars, i - 1]`, **and**
  - `C[i].high > C[j].high` for every `j` in `[i + 1, i + right_bars]`.

- Candle `i` is a **support pivot** iff:
  - `C[i].low < C[j].low` for every `j` in `[i - left_bars, i - 1]`, **and**
  - `C[i].low < C[j].low` for every `j` in `[i + 1, i + right_bars]`.

The rule is **strict** on both sides — a candle that merely ties with
its neighbours is not a pivot. It is the same comparison TradingView
uses, so the Python output and the Pine Script plot stay in lockstep.

### The confirmation delay

A pivot at index `i` cannot be known at `i`. It becomes known only
after the last bar of the `right_bars` window has closed:

    confirmation_index = pivot_index + right_bars

That bar — **not** the pivot bar — is the first moment the level is
actionable. This is the reason `sr_levels.json` stores two timestamps
per level (`pivot_time` and `confirmed_time`).

### Worked example

Suppose `left_bars = 15`, `right_bars = 15`, and a support pivot
appears at bar 2577 with a low of `22454.45`.

- The 15 bars before bar 2577 (bars 2562..2576) all have `low > 22454.45`.
- The 15 bars after bar 2577 (bars 2578..2592) all have `low > 22454.45`.
- Therefore bar 2577 is a support pivot.
- It is confirmed at bar `2577 + 15 = 2592`.

Recorded:

```json
{
  "level": "support",
  "price": 22454.45,
  "pivot_bar_index": 2577,
  "pivot_time": "2026-10-23T14:42:00+05:30",
  "confirmed_bar_index": 2592,
  "confirmed_time": "2026-10-23T14:57:00+05:30",
  "left_bars": 15,
  "right_bars": 15
}
