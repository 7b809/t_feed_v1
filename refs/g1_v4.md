# G1 Unified Strategy Specification: Cross-Index Relational Isolation & Milestone Execution Engine

**Document Version:** 5.1.0  
**Status:** Approved for Implementation  
**Scope:** Multi-Index Boundary Surveillance, Dynamic Strike Isolation, Dual-Phase SR Entry Resolution (Pre-09:30 Backward-Scan with Prior-Day Lookback vs. Post-09:30 Forward-Scan), Isolation-Candle Close Entry Execution, First-Candle Range Gating, Fixed-Floor SL, $R_3$ Milestone Previous-Close Trailing, and Dynamic $15/30 \to 15/3$ Resistance Exit.

---

## 1. Executive Architecture & End-to-End Workflow

The strategy combines multi-index cross-boundary surveillance with a timing-aware SR entry engine and progressive milestone position management:

1. **Surveillance & Isolation Engine:** Tracks 4 benchmark indices on a prioritized multi-index watch. Upon the first boundary hit ($R_3$ or $S_3$), the engine establishes directional bias and isolates the corresponding At-The-Money (ATM) NIFTY 50 options pair (Primary bias leg and Counter leg).
2. **Dual-Phase SR Resolution Engine:**
   - **Early Isolation ($09:15 \le T_{\text{iso}} < 09:30$):** Checks backward candles before $09:30$. If intraday candles are insufficient to fulfill the left-lookback ($L=15$), the previous trading day's tail candles are back-filled. If an SR pivot was already confirmed at or prior to $T_{\text{iso}}$, the order is executed immediately on the **isolation-time candle close**. If no prior SR confirmed, it transitions to forward surveillance.
   - **Post-09:30 Isolation ($T_{\text{iso}} \ge 09:30$) or Early Fallback:** Evaluates forward candles where **both pivot time and confirmation time occur strictly after** $T_{\text{iso}}$. Order executes on the confirmation bar.
3. **Order Placement & Position Management Engine:** Validates entry against the execution instrument's opening candle range ($L_{\text{FC}} \le \text{Entry} \le H_{\text{FC}}$), establishes a capital-protective stop-loss ($\max(30\%, 30\text{ pts})$), and dynamically monitors the $R_3$ milestone trigger to lock in profits at the previous candle's close while tightening resistance exit sensitivity from $15/30$ to $15/3$.

## 3. Mathematical Foundations

### 3.1 Strike Normalization
When a boundary touch is detected at timestamp $T_{\text{touch}}$, the NIFTY 50 spot price $S_{\text{NIFTY}}$ is mapped to the nearest round-50 ATM strike:

$$
\text{Strike}_{\text{ATM}} = \text{round}\left(\frac{S_{\text{NIFTY}}}{50}\right) \times 50
$$

### 3.2 Opening Range (OR) Floor-Trader Ladder
For any asset, the opening window candles define the price boundaries:

$$
O = \text{bar}_0.\text{open}, \quad H = \max_{t \in \text{OR}}(\text{bar}_t.\text{high}), \quad L = \min_{t \in \text{OR}}(\text{bar}_t.\text{low}), \quad C = \text{bar}_{\text{last}}.\text{close}
$$

$$
\text{AVG} = \frac{H + L}{2}, \quad P = \frac{H + L + C}{3}
$$

$$
R_1 = 2P - L, \quad R_2 = P + (H - L), \quad R_3 = H + 2(P - L), \quad R_4 = H + 3(P - L)
$$

$$
S_1 = 2P - H, \quad S_2 = P - (H - L), \quad S_3 = L - 2(H - P), \quad S_4 = L - 3(H - P)
$$

### 3.3 Initial Stop-Loss (Fixed-Floor Premium Rule)
$$
\Delta_{\text{SL}} = \max\left(0.30 \times \text{Entry},\ 30.0\right)
$$

$$
\text{SL}_{\text{initial}} = \text{Entry} - \Delta_{\text{SL}}
$$

### 3.4 Entry Zone Gating for $R_3$ Milestone
A trade is classified as an inside-zone entry if placed within the lower quartile balance:

$$
S_2 \le \text{Entry} \le \text{AVG}
$$

### 3.5 $R_3$ Milestone Trigger Conditions
On any bar $j > \text{entry\_idx}$, the trigger transitions $\text{sl\_moved} \to \text{True}$ if and only if:

1. $\text{bar}_j.\text{high} \ge R_3$
2. $\text{entry\_is\_in\_zone} = \text{True}$
3. $\left(\text{bar}_j.\text{high} - O\right) > 50.0$

## 4. Operational Pipeline & SR Entry Timing Rules

### 4.1 Stage 1 & 2: Surveillance and Relational Isolation
* Indices are polled every 2 seconds. The first index to cross $R_3$ or $S_3$ triggers isolation.
* Tie-breaking hierarchy:
  $$\text{MIDCPNIFTY} \succ \text{BANKNIFTY} \succ \text{NIFTY} \succ \text{SENSEX}$$
* Directional bias assignment:
  * Index touches $R_3 \implies \text{Bias} = \text{CE}$ (Primary: ATM CE, Counter: ATM PE)
  * Index touches $S_3 \implies \text{Bias} = \text{PE}$ (Primary: ATM PE, Counter: ATM CE)

### 4.2 Stage 3: Dual-Phase SR Detection & Entry Timing
Only the **Primary Bias Instrument** is scanned for SR levels. Once a level confirms, the trade leg is mapped:
* **Support Confirmed** $\implies$ BUY **Primary Bias Contract**. Traded OR is $\text{OR}_{\text{Primary}}$.
* **Resistance Confirmed** $\implies$ BUY **Counter Contract**. Traded OR is $\text{OR}_{\text{Counter}}$.

#### Phase A: Early Isolation ($09:15 \le T_{\text{iso}} < 09:30$)
1. **Backward Lookback & Prior-Day Stitching:**  
   The scanner looks through candles available up to $T_{\text{iso}}$. Because $L=15$ bars are required to confirm a pivot on the left, intraday bars before $09:30$ are insufficient on their own (e.g., at $09:20$, only 5 bars exist). The engine automatically prepends the prior trading day's closing 1-minute bars ($15:16 \dots 15:30$) to satisfy $L=15$.
2. **Retrospective SR Hit:**  
   If an SR level was confirmed at or prior to $T_{\text{iso}}$ ($\text{confirm\_ts} \le T_{\text{iso}}$):
   * An entry signal is emitted immediately.
   * **Execution Rule:** The order is placed on the **isolation-time candle close**.
   * $\text{Entry Price} = \text{candles}[\text{iso\_idx}].\text{close}$ of the execution contract.
   * $\text{Entry Index} = \text{iso\_idx}$.
3. **Early Fallback (No Prior SR Confirmed):**  
   If no SR level was confirmed at or before $T_{\text{iso}}$:
   * The scanner switches to forward mode.
   * A valid SR must satisfy: **$\text{pivot\_ts} > T_{\text{iso}}$ AND $\text{confirm\_ts} > T_{\text{iso}}$**.
   * Execution occurs on the confirmation bar: $\text{Entry Price} = \text{candles}[\text{confirm\_idx}].\text{open}$ (or close of confirm bar).

#### Phase B: Standard Isolation ($T_{\text{iso}} \ge 09:30$)
* No retrospective lookback is executed.
* The engine only monitors forward SR levels where **$\text{pivot\_ts} > T_{\text{iso}}$ AND $\text{confirm\_ts} > T_{\text{iso}}$**.
* Entry is taken at the confirmation bar open/close.

### 4.3 Stage 4: First-Candle Range Gating
Before arming any order on the target contract, verify against the execution contract's own $09:15$ opening bar:
$$L_{\text{FC}} \le \text{Entry} \le H_{\text{FC}}$$
If $\text{Entry} < L_{\text{FC}}$ or $\text{Entry} > H_{\text{FC}}$, the order is discarded with status `skipped: outside_first_candle_range`.

### 4.4 Stage 5 & 6: Position Management & Dynamic Exit
* Initial Stop-Loss is established: $\text{SL}_{\text{initial}} = \text{Entry} - \max(0.30 \times \text{Entry}, 30)$.
* Scanner starts at $15/30$.
* For each subsequent bar $j > \text{entry\_idx}$:
  1. **SL Breach Check (Priority 1):** If $\text{bar}_j.\text{low} \le \text{SL}_{\text{current}}$, exit at $\text{SL}_{\text{current}}$.
  2. **$R_3$ Milestone Check:** If $\text{bar}_j.\text{high} \ge R_3$, entry was in $[S_2 \dots \text{AVG}]$, and move from open $> 50$:
     * Ratchet SL to previous candle close: $\text{SL}_{\text{current}} := \text{candles}[j-1].\text{close}$.
     * Flip scanner parameters: $\text{left} = 15, \text{right} = 3$.
  3. **Resistance Target Check:** Using active configuration ($15/30 \to 15/3$), if resistance pivot above entry confirms at bar $j$, exit at $\text{bar}_j.\text{close}$.
  4. **EOD Exit:** Square off at $15:30$ IST close.

## 5. Complete Implementation Engine

```python
"""
Unified Strategy Engine: Cross-Index Isolation, Timing-Aware SR Entry, & Milestone Execution
"""

from dataclasses import dataclass
from datetime import datetime, time
from typing import Dict, List, Optional, Tuple, Any

# ==============================================================================
# DATA STRUCTURES
# ==============================================================================

@dataclass
class Candle:
    ts: datetime
    open: float
    high: float
    low: float
    close: float
    volume: int = 0

@dataclass
class SRPivot:
    level_type: str         # "support" | "resistance"
    price: float
    pivot_idx: int
    pivot_ts: datetime
    confirmed_idx: int
    confirm_ts: datetime
    source: str             # "PRE_ISO_CONFIRMED" | "POST_ISO_FORWARD"
    entry_mode: str         # "AT_ISOLATION_CLOSE" | "AT_CONFIRMATION"

@dataclass
class IsolationRecord:
    trigger_index: str
    trigger_boundary: str  # "R3" | "S3"
    touched_at: datetime
    trigger_is_self_nifty: bool
    isolated_strike: int
    primary_bias: str      # "CE" | "PE"
    primary_key: str
    opposite_bias: str     # "PE" | "CE"
    opposite_key: str

# ==============================================================================
# STAGE 1 & 2: ISOLATION MODULE
# ==============================================================================

class IsolationEngine:
    def __init__(self, config: Dict[str, Any]):
        self.cfg = config
        self.priority_map = {
            item["symbol"]: item["priority"]
            for item in config["MONITORED_INDICES"]
        }

    def compute_or_levels(self, candles: List[Candle]) -> Dict[str, float]:
        if not candles:
            raise ValueError("No candles provided for Opening Range computation")
        
        o = candles[0].open
        h = max(c.high for c in candles)
        l = min(c.low for c in candles)
        c = candles[-1].close

        avg = (h + l) / 2.0
        p = (h + l + c) / 3.0

        return {
            "O": o, "H": h, "L": l, "C": c,
            "AVG": avg, "P": p,
            "R1": 2 * p - l,
            "R2": p + (h - l),
            "R3": h + 2 * (p - l),
            "R4": h + 3 * (p - l),
            "S1": 2 * p - h,
            "S2": p - (h - l),
            "S3": l - 2 * (h - p),
            "S4": l - 3 * (h - p),
        }

    def detect_first_boundary_touch(
        self,
        index_ticks: Dict[str, List[Tuple[datetime, float]]],
        index_or_levels: Dict[str, Dict[str, float]]
    ) -> Optional[Tuple[str, str, datetime]]:
        breaches = []
        for symbol, ticks in index_ticks.items():
            orl = index_or_levels.get(symbol)
            if not orl:
                continue
            for ts, ltp in ticks:
                if ltp >= orl["R3"]:
                    breaches.append((ts, self.priority_map[symbol], symbol, "R3"))
                    break
                elif ltp <= orl["S3"]:
                    breaches.append((ts, self.priority_map[symbol], symbol, "S3"))
                    break

        if not breaches:
            return None

        breaches.sort(key=lambda x: (x[0], x[1]))
        winning_touch = breaches[0]
        return winning_touch[2], winning_touch[3], winning_touch[0]

    def isolate_strikes(
        self,
        trigger_symbol: str,
        boundary: str,
        touch_time: datetime,
        nifty_spot_ltp: float,
        contract_lookup: Any
    ) -> IsolationRecord:
        atm_strike = int(round(nifty_spot_ltp / 50.0) * 50)
        is_self_nifty = (trigger_symbol == "NIFTY")

        if boundary == "R3":
            primary_bias, opposite_bias = "CE", "PE"
        else:
            primary_bias, opposite_bias = "PE", "CE"

        primary_key = contract_lookup.get_key("NIFTY", atm_strike, primary_bias)
        opposite_key = contract_lookup.get_key("NIFTY", atm_strike, opposite_bias)

        return IsolationRecord(
            trigger_index=trigger_symbol,
            trigger_boundary=boundary,
            touched_at=touch_time,
            trigger_is_self_nifty=is_self_nifty,
            isolated_strike=atm_strike,
            primary_bias=primary_bias,
            primary_key=primary_key,
            opposite_bias=opposite_bias,
            opposite_key=opposite_key,
        )

# ==============================================================================
# STAGE 3, 4 & 5: TIMING-AWARE SR DETECTION & POSITION RUNNER
# ==============================================================================

class ExecutionEngine:
    def __init__(self, config: Dict[str, Any]):
        self.cfg = config

    def is_pivot(self, candles: List[Candle], idx: int, left: int, right: int, ptype: str) -> bool:
        if idx < left or idx + right >= len(candles):
            return False
        target_val = candles[idx].high if ptype == "resistance" else candles[idx].low

        for j in range(idx - left, idx):
            comp = candles[j].high if ptype == "resistance" else candles[j].low
            if ptype == "resistance" and comp >= target_val:
                return False
            if ptype == "support" and comp <= target_val:
                return False

        for j in range(idx + 1, idx + right + 1):
            comp = candles[j].high if ptype == "resistance" else candles[j].low
            if ptype == "resistance" and comp >= target_val:
                return False
            if ptype == "support" and comp <= target_val:
                return False

        return True

    def find_entry_sr(
        self,
        candles: List[Candle],
        isolation_time: datetime,
        prior_day_candles: Optional[List[Candle]] = None
    ) -> Optional[SRPivot]:
        """
        Implements timing-aware dual-phase SR resolution:
        1. If isolation_time < 09:30:
           - Look back through candles up to isolation_time (using prior_day_candles for lookback).
           - If SR confirmed <= isolation_time -> order on ISOLATION CANDLE CLOSE.
           - Else -> forward scan where pivot_ts > isolation_time AND confirm_ts > isolation_time.
        2. If isolation_time >= 09:30:
           - Forward scan only: pivot_ts > isolation_time AND confirm_ts > isolation_time.
        """
        left = self.cfg["SR_LEFT"]
        right = self.cfg["SR_RIGHT"]
        is_early = isolation_time.time() < time(9, 30)

        # ----------------------------------------------------------------------
        # PHASE 1: RETROSPECTIVE CHECK (Early isolation only: 09:15 - 09:30)
        # ----------------------------------------------------------------------
        if is_early and self.cfg.get("EARLY_SR_ALLOW_PRIOR_DAY", True):
            # Combine prior day candles to satisfy the left lookback (15 bars)
            history = (prior_day_candles or []) + candles
            offset = len(prior_day_candles or [])

            # Locate the isolation candle index in intraday candles
            iso_idx = next(
                (i for i, c in enumerate(candles) if c.ts >= isolation_time),
                len(candles) - 1
            )
            max_check_idx = offset + iso_idx

            # Scan backwards for confirmed pivots confirmed at or before isolation_time
            candidate_pivots: List[SRPivot] = []
            for i in range(offset, max_check_idx - right + 1):
                conf_idx = i + right
                if conf_idx > max_check_idx:
                    break

                conf_candle = history[conf_idx]
                if conf_candle.ts > isolation_time:
                    continue

                bar = history[i]
                if self.is_pivot(history, i, left, right, "support"):
                    candidate_pivots.append(SRPivot(
                        level_type="support",
                        price=bar.low,
                        pivot_idx=i - offset,
                        pivot_ts=bar.ts,
                        confirmed_idx=conf_idx - offset,
                        confirm_ts=conf_candle.ts,
                        source="PRE_ISO_CONFIRMED",
                        entry_mode="AT_ISOLATION_CLOSE"
                    ))
                elif self.is_pivot(history, i, left, right, "resistance"):
                    candidate_pivots.append(SRPivot(
                        level_type="resistance",
                        price=bar.high,
                        pivot_idx=i - offset,
                        pivot_ts=bar.ts,
                        confirmed_idx=conf_idx - offset,
                        confirm_ts=conf_candle.ts,
                        source="PRE_ISO_CONFIRMED",
                        entry_mode="AT_ISOLATION_CLOSE"
                    ))

            if candidate_pivots:
                # Return the most recently confirmed SR before isolation
                candidate_pivots.sort(key=lambda p: p.confirm_ts)
                return candidate_pivots[-1]

        # ----------------------------------------------------------------------
        # PHASE 2: FORWARD SCAN (Post-09:30 OR Early Fallback)
        # Both pivot_ts and confirm_ts must be strictly AFTER isolation_time
        # ----------------------------------------------------------------------
        for i in range(left, len(candles) - right):
            bar = candles[i]
            conf_bar = candles[i + right]

            # Enforce strictly post-isolation pivot AND confirmation
            if bar.ts <= isolation_time or conf_bar.ts <= isolation_time:
                continue

            if self.is_pivot(candles, i, left, right, "support"):
                return SRPivot(
                    level_type="support",
                    price=bar.low,
                    pivot_idx=i,
                    pivot_ts=bar.ts,
                    confirmed_idx=i + right,
                    confirm_ts=conf_bar.ts,
                    source="POST_ISO_FORWARD",
                    entry_mode="AT_CONFIRMATION"
                )
            elif self.is_pivot(candles, i, left, right, "resistance"):
                return SRPivot(
                    level_type="resistance",
                    price=bar.high,
                    pivot_idx=i,
                    pivot_ts=bar.ts,
                    confirmed_idx=i + right,
                    confirm_ts=conf_bar.ts,
                    source="POST_ISO_FORWARD",
                    entry_mode="AT_CONFIRMATION"
                )

        return None

    def compute_initial_sl(self, entry: float) -> Tuple[float, float, str]:
        pct_offset = entry * self.cfg["SL_PCT_OF_ENTRY"]
        pts_offset = self.cfg["SL_MIN_POINTS"]
        offset = max(pct_offset, pts_offset)
        basis = "PCT_30" if pct_offset >= pts_offset else "MIN_30"
        return entry - offset, offset, basis

    def entry_is_in_zone(self, entry: float, orl: Dict[str, float]) -> bool:
        return orl[self.cfg["ENTRY_ZONE_LOW_LEVEL"]] <= entry <= orl[self.cfg["ENTRY_ZONE_HIGH_LEVEL"]]

    def check_r3_trigger(self, bar: Candle, orl: Dict[str, float], entry: float) -> bool:
        if bar.high < orl["R3"]:
            return False
        if self.cfg["TRIGGER_REQUIRES_ENTRY_ZONE"] and not self.entry_is_in_zone(entry, orl):
            return False
        ref = orl["O"] if self.cfg["TRIGGER_POINTS_FROM"] == "OR_OPEN" else orl["L"]
        return (bar.high - ref) > self.cfg["R3_POINTS_TRIGGER"]

    def find_resistance_target(
        self,
        candles: List[Candle],
        from_idx: int,
        entry: float,
        left: int,
        right: int
    ) -> Optional[Dict[str, Any]]:
        for i in range(from_idx + 1, len(candles) - right):
            if self.is_pivot(candles, i, left, right, "resistance"):
                if candles[i].high > entry:
                    return {
                        "level": candles[i].high,
                        "pivot_idx": i,
                        "pivot_ts": candles[i].ts,
                        "confirmed_idx": i + right,
                        "confirm_ts": candles[i + right].ts
                    }
        return None

    def run_position(
        self,
        candles: List[Candle],
        signal: SRPivot,
        isolation_time: datetime,
        orl: Dict[str, float],
        first_candle: Candle,
        leg_name: str
    ) -> Dict[str, Any]:
        """
        Executes single order state machine with execution price selected
        according to signal entry_mode.
        """
        # Determine execution entry bar & price
        if signal.entry_mode == "AT_ISOLATION_CLOSE":
            # Early retrospective hit: execute on isolation candle close
            iso_idx = next(
                (i for i, c in enumerate(candles) if c.ts >= isolation_time),
                len(candles) - 1
            )
            entry_idx = iso_idx
            entry_price = candles[entry_idx].close
            entry_time = candles[entry_idx].ts
        else:
            # Forward confirmation: execute on confirmation bar
            entry_idx = signal.confirmed_idx
            entry_price = candles[entry_idx].close  # or candles[entry_idx].open as configured
            entry_time = candles[entry_idx].ts

        # Stage 4: First-Candle Range Gate Check
        if self.cfg["ENTRY_REQUIRE_IN_FIRST_CANDLE"]:
            if not (first_candle.low <= entry_price <= first_candle.high):
                return {
                    "status": "SKIPPED",
                    "reason": "outside_first_candle_range",
                    "entry_price": entry_price,
                    "fc_low": first_candle.low,
                    "fc_high": first_candle.high,
                }

        # Stage 5: Compute Initial Risk
        initial_sl, sl_offset, sl_basis = self.compute_initial_sl(entry_price)
        cur_sl = initial_sl
        cur_left = self.cfg["EXIT_LEFT_INITIAL"]
        cur_right = self.cfg["EXIT_RIGHT_INITIAL"]

        in_zone = self.entry_is_in_zone(entry_price, orl)
        sl_moved = False

        log = {
            "status": "CLOSED",
            "leg": leg_name,
            "signal_source": signal.source,
            "entry_mode": signal.entry_mode,
            "entry_price": entry_price,
            "entry_idx": entry_idx,
            "entry_time": entry_time,
            "entry_in_zone": in_zone,
            "initial_sl": initial_sl,
            "sl_basis": sl_basis,
            "sl_offset": sl_offset,
            "current_sl": cur_sl,
            "sl_moved": False,
            "trigger_ts": None,
            "trigger_prev_close": None,
            "exit_config": {"left": cur_left, "right": cur_right},
            "exit_price": None,
            "exit_idx": None,
            "exit_time": None,
            "reason": None,
            "pnl_points": 0.0,
            "pnl_rupees": 0.0,
        }

        # Stage 6: Bar-by-Bar Lifecycle Progression
        for j in range(entry_idx + 1, len(candles)):
            bar = candles[j]

            # 1. Conservative Stop-Loss Check (Priority 1)
            if bar.low <= log["current_sl"]:
                log["exit_price"] = log["current_sl"]
                log["exit_idx"] = j
                log["exit_time"] = bar.ts
                log["reason"] = "SL" if not sl_moved else "PREV_CLOSE_SL"
                break

            # 2. R3 Milestone Check
            if (not sl_moved) and self.check_r3_trigger(bar, orl, entry_price):
                prev_close = candles[j - 1].close
                log["current_sl"] = prev_close
                log["sl_moved"] = True
                sl_moved = True
                log["trigger_ts"] = bar.ts
                log["trigger_prev_close"] = prev_close

                # Dynamic Exit Scanner Flip
                cur_left = self.cfg["EXIT_LEFT_POST_MOVE"]
                cur_right = self.cfg["EXIT_RIGHT_POST_MOVE"]
                log["exit_config"] = {"left": cur_left, "right": cur_right}

            # 3. Dynamic Resistance Confirmation Check
            res = self.find_resistance_target(candles, j, entry_price, cur_left, cur_right)
            if res is not None and res["confirmed_idx"] == j:
                log["exit_price"] = bar.close
                log["exit_idx"] = j
                log["exit_time"] = bar.ts
                log["reason"] = f"RESISTANCE_CONFIRM_{cur_left}_{cur_right}@{res['level']}"
                break

        # 4. EOD Fallback Exit
        if log["exit_price"] is None:
            last_bar = candles[-1]
            log["exit_price"] = last_bar.close
            log["exit_idx"] = len(candles) - 1
            log["exit_time"] = last_bar.ts
            log["reason"] = "EOD"

        log["pnl_points"] = log["exit_price"] - log["entry_price"]
        log["pnl_rupees"] = log["pnl_points"] * self.cfg["LOT"]
        return log

class UnifiedStrategyPipeline:
    def __init__(self, config: Dict[str, Any], contract_lookup: Any):
        self.cfg = config
        self.contract_lookup = contract_lookup
        self.isolation_engine = IsolationEngine(config)
        self.execution_engine = ExecutionEngine(config)

    def execute_session(
        self,
        index_ticks: Dict[str, List[Tuple[datetime, float]]],
        index_or_candles: Dict[str, List[Candle]],
        nifty_spot_ticks: List[Tuple[datetime, float]],
        options_candles: Dict[str, List[Candle]],
        prior_day_options_candles: Optional[Dict[str, List[Candle]]] = None
    ) -> Dict[str, Any]:
        """
        Executes end-to-end trading session:
        Surveillance -> Relational Strike Isolation -> Timing-Aware SR Detection ->
        First-Candle Range Gate -> Single Managed Order.
        """
        # Step 1: Pre-market Index OR Levels
        index_or_levels = {
            sym: self.isolation_engine.compute_or_levels(candles)
            for sym, candles in index_or_candles.items()
        }

        # Step 2: Surveillance Boundary Touch
        touch = self.isolation_engine.detect_first_boundary_touch(index_ticks, index_or_levels)
        if not touch:
            return {"status": "NO_TRADE", "reason": "No index boundary touch detected"}

        trigger_sym, boundary, touch_time = touch

        # Locate NIFTY Spot LTP at touch millisecond
        spot_at_touch = next((ltp for ts, ltp in nifty_spot_ticks if ts >= touch_time), None)
        if spot_at_touch is None:
            spot_at_touch = nifty_spot_ticks[-1][1]

        # Step 3: Relational Strike Isolation
        iso_record = self.isolation_engine.isolate_strikes(
            trigger_sym, boundary, touch_time, spot_at_touch, self.contract_lookup
        )

        # Step 4: Resolve SR Entry on Primary Bias Contract
        primary_candles = options_candles[iso_record.primary_key]
        prior_day_primary = (prior_day_options_candles or {}).get(iso_record.primary_key)

        signal = self.execution_engine.find_entry_sr(
            candles=primary_candles,
            isolation_time=iso_record.touched_at,
            prior_day_candles=prior_day_primary
        )

        if not signal:
            return {
                "status": "NO_TRADE",
                "isolation": iso_record,
                "reason": "No valid SR level confirmed (neither retrospective nor post-isolation)"
            }

        # Step 5: Route to Traded Leg
        if signal.level_type == "support":
            traded_key = iso_record.primary_key
            leg_name = "PRIMARY_BIAS"
        else:
            traded_key = iso_record.opposite_key
            leg_name = "COUNTER_BIAS"

        traded_candles = options_candles[traded_key]
        traded_or_candles = traded_candles[:15]  # 09:15 to 09:30 window
        traded_or_levels = self.isolation_engine.compute_or_levels(traded_or_candles)
        first_candle = traded_candles[0]

        # Step 6: Execute Position Runner
        trade_log = self.execution_engine.run_position(
            candles=traded_candles,
            signal=signal,
            isolation_time=iso_record.touched_at,
            orl=traded_or_levels,
            first_candle=first_candle,
            leg_name=leg_name
        )

        return {
            "status": "COMPLETED",
            "isolation": iso_record,
            "signal": signal,
            "trade": trade_log
        }
```

## 7. Strategy Invariants & Operational Failsafes

1. **Isolation-Bar Close Anchor:** When an early retrospective SR is confirmed prior to or at isolation time, the trade price is anchored at the exact **isolation candle close**, preventing backtest lookahead bias while capitalizing on already-established opening structures.
2. **Prior-Day Continuity:** Intraday morning bars before $09:30$ are never discarded for lack of length; stitching the previous day's closing session guarantees that $L=15$ pivot geometries are statistically robust.
3. **Forward Timestamp Independence:** For any trade generated outside the retrospective window, both $\text{pivot\_ts}$ and $\text{confirm\_ts}$ must strictly exceed $T_{\text{iso}}$, completely prohibiting post-09:30 trades from reacting to stale pre-market or early-session pivots.
4. **First-Candle Range Enforcement:** Even if a retrospective SR triggers at isolation close, the entry price must respect $L_{\text{FC}} \le \text{Entry} \le H_{\text{FC}}$. Overextended gap moves are rejected.
5. **Conservative Stop-Loss Priority:** If a single 1-minute candle breaches the stop-loss and confirms a target simultaneously, the stop-loss is prioritized.
